Guide

Turn that old tablet in the drawer into a family calendar.

You don’t need to buy a smart display. An Android tablet you already own can show the week, today’s to-dos, the weather and the shopping list on the kitchen wall. Here is what works and how to set it up in about ten minutes.

What you need

  • An Android tablet with Google Play, running Android 8 or newer. Almost any tablet from the last several years qualifies.
  • A Google account, for the calendar and to-do list. Other calendars (Outlook, Apple, school and team calendars) can be added with a link.
  • A charger within reach of where it will hang or stand.

Amazon Fire tablets don’t work, because they lack Google’s sign-in. iPads aren’t supported yet; an iPad version is planned.

Set it up

  1. Install Skreen from Google Play and sign in with Google.
  2. Choose your calendars and a to-do list in Settings. Add anything else with a link: school and team calendars work the same way.
  3. Pick your city for the weather.
  4. Plug it in and mount it. A stand or wall mount that keeps the charger connected is easy to find. Skreen dims at night and nudges the layout through the day, so the screen isn’t showing one frozen image for months.

What goes on the screen

For free: a big clock, weather, your schedule, a to-do list that syncs with Google Tasks (so anyone can add one from a phone), timers, music controls and live radio. With the optional Skreen+ upgrade, every person gets their own color-coded column; you also get chores that earn stars, a sticky-note whiteboard, a meal planner, a shopping list, and a Big Skreen Mode with large slides readable from across the room.

Things worth knowing

  • No Skreen account. Your calendar and lists stay in your Google account and on the tablet. There’s no ad tracking.
  • Adding events: add them to your calendar the way you already do, and they appear within a few minutes. To-dos can be added on the wall, by voice, or from your phone.
  • Not a voice assistant. Skreen doesn’t control smart-home devices or make video calls. It’s the screen part, done bigger.

Questions

Will any old tablet work?

Any Android tablet with Google Play, running Android 8 or newer, and a Google account. Amazon Fire tablets don’t work, because they don’t have Google’s sign-in. An iPad version of Skreen is planned but isn’t available yet.

Is it safe to leave an old tablet plugged in all the time?

Most modern tablets handle it fine, and many manage their charging to protect the battery. A very old tablet with a swollen or damaged battery should not be left charging. If the case bulges or the screen lifts, stop using it.

Does Skreen cost anything?

The main display is free with no ads. Skreen+ is an optional one-time $14.99 purchase (launch price) that adds family columns, chores, a whiteboard, a meal planner and more. There is no subscription.

Can the whole family see their own schedule on one screen?

Yes, with Skreen+. Each person gets a color-coded column with their own calendar and to-dos, side by side.
