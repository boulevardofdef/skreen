Guide

Put the school and sports calendars on your wall.

Practice times, early dismissals, lunch menus and game days are usually trapped in a school portal or a team app. Most of them can publish a calendar link, and Skreen can show that link on the wall next to everything else. No extra sign-in.

The 60-second version

  1. Find the school, team or club calendar and look for Subscribe, iCal, Export or Calendar feed.
  2. Copy the link. It ends in .ics or starts with webcal://. Either works.
  3. In Skreen, open Settings → Other calendars, paste the link and tap Add calendar. Give it a name like “Lincoln Elementary” if you like.

It then appears in your schedule, color-coded, beside your Google calendars. You can add up to eight.

Where to look

  • School and district websites. Many have a calendar page with a Subscribe or Add to calendar button. Right-click or long-press it and copy the link address. On a phone the button may try to open a calendar app; copying the link is what you want.
  • Team and club apps. Apps such as TeamSnap and SportsEngine generally offer a calendar feed or an iCal subscribe link in their calendar settings. Menu names change, so search the app’s help for “calendar feed” or “iCal”.
  • Google Calendar. If the school shares a Google calendar, you don’t need a link at all. Add it to your account and tick it in Skreen.
  • Outlook or iCloud calendars. Both can publish a link; see the in-app guide under Settings → Other calendars.

Can’t find a link? Email the school office or the coach and ask: “Is there an iCal or subscribable calendar feed?” If they have one, they can send you the link in a sentence.

Give each child their own column

With Skreen+ you can add each family member once, with their own calendars and to-do list, and see everyone side by side. A school calendar link added to Skreen shows in the main schedule; the family columns are built from your Google calendars and tasks.

Questions

My school only has a PDF or a website calendar. Can I still use it?

Only if the page offers a Subscribe, iCal or Export option. A PDF or a plain web page can’t be read by any calendar app. Ask the school office whether a subscribable calendar exists; many have one.

Will the school calendar update on its own?

Yes. Skreen re-reads the link regularly. Changes can take a few minutes to appear, and some providers refresh their published links only every few hours.

Is the link private?

Treat it like a password: anyone who has it can read that calendar. Skreen stores links on your tablet only, and its relay fetches the calendar without keeping it.

Can Skreen change events on a school calendar?

No. Calendars added by link are read-only.
