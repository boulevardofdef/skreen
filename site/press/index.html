In one paragraph

Skreen turns an Android tablet you already own into an always-on wall display for your day: calendar, to-do list, weather, timers, music and radio. It is free, with a single optional $14.99 one-time upgrade (Skreen+) that adds a column for each family member, chores with stars, a sticky-note whiteboard, a meal planner and a Big Skreen Mode readable from across the room. There’s no hardware to buy and no subscription, and Skreen has no accounts or servers for your calendar: it goes from Google to the tablet.

Quick facts

  • Name: Skreen
  • Maker: Logflume, an independent developer
  • Platform: Android tablets with Google Play, Android 8 or newer. Not Amazon Fire tablets. An iPad version is planned.
  • Price: Free. Skreen+ is a one-time $14.99 purchase (launch price), with no subscription.
  • Availability: Google Play
  • Privacy: No Skreen accounts, no ads. Calendars and lists come from the user’s Google account straight to the tablet. Privacy policy
  • Try it without installing: sample-data demo in a browser
  • Contact: support@skreen.fyi
