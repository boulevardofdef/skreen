The wall display that stays on.

Put a cheap Android tablet on the wall and Skreen turns it into the one screen the whole house looks at: the time, the weather, today’s calendar and your to-do list. Big, calm and always current.

Everything a wall display should do. Free.

The main display is free forever. No account with us, no ads, no tracking.

01

Clock and weather

A big, easy-to-read clock, the next few hours and the week ahead, and a plain-English line on whether it will rain.

02

Your calendars

Google Calendar, plus Outlook, Apple, Yahoo or any calendar link. Today and tomorrow at a glance, with what’s on right now highlighted.

03

A to-do list for everyone

Your Google Tasks list, on the wall. Tap to check things off, add new ones by typing or talking, and tag who it’s for.

04

Timers

Up to four named countdowns, set with a keypad or your voice. When one finishes the whole display turns orange and the tablet rings.

05

Music

See what’s playing from Spotify, YouTube Music or any music app, with play, pause and skip. Or search internet radio and play it right on the display.

06

Made to stay on

Night mode, a slow layout drift and a rotating big-clock tour help protect the screen. Dimming and brightness are built in.

Timers right under the clock

Start one from the little button in the corner. They stay where you can see them.

Music without another app

Controls for what’s playing, and radio you can search and start yourself.

Your data stays yours.

Your calendar and tasks go straight from Google to your tablet. They don’t pass through us and we don’t store them. There are no ads and no analytics, and we never sell or share your information. Read the privacy policy.
