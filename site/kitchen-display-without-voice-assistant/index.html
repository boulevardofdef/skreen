Guide

A kitchen screen that isn’t listening.

Smart displays bundle a screen with a voice assistant, a microphone and often a camera. If all you want is the calendar, the weather and the to-do list, you can have the screen without the rest.

What Skreen does and doesn’t do

  • No voice assistant. Skreen never listens for a wake word. The only time it uses the microphone is when you tap the mic button to dictate a to-do or a timer, using Android’s own speech recognition.
  • No ads and no tracking. The main display is free with no ads, and Skreen has no accounts or analytics profile of you.
  • No smart-home control and no video calls. It’s the screen part only.
  • Your calendar stays yours. Events come from your Google account straight to the tablet. Skreen has no server holding your calendar.

What you get instead

A big clock, the weather, your schedule and a to-do list that syncs with Google Tasks (so anyone can add one from a phone), plus timers, now-playing music controls and live radio. With the optional Skreen+ upgrade, a column for each family member, chores, a whiteboard and a meal planner.

What you give up

You can’t ask it questions, turn on lights by voice or make a video call. If you want those things, a smart display does them well. If you don’t, you can skip the always-on microphone.

Android itself may still have Google’s voice features on the tablet. If you want to be sure, check your tablet’s voice and assistant settings. Skreen doesn’t turn them on or off.

Questions

Does Skreen have a microphone on all the time?

No. It uses the microphone only when you tap the mic button to dictate a to-do or timer.

Does Skreen show ads or track me?

No ads. Skreen has no accounts, and your calendar goes from Google to the tablet without passing through a Skreen server.

Can I use it without Alexa or Google Assistant?

Yes. Skreen is an app, not an assistant. It needs an Android tablet with Google Play and a Google account.
