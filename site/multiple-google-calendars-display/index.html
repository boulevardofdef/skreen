Guide

Everyone’s Google Calendar, on one wall.

Most families keep several calendars: one for each adult, one for the kids, one for the school, one shared. On a phone they hide behind menus. On the wall, they can all show at once.

In free Skreen

  • Sign in with Google and tick the calendars to show. They merge into one color-coded schedule: today, tomorrow and the days ahead.
  • Add calendars from other services, like Outlook or iCloud, or a school or team calendar, with a link.

With Skreen+

  • A column for each person. Add each person once, with their own calendars and to-do list, and see everyone side by side in their own color.
  • Several Google accounts. Work, personal and a partner’s account can feed the same screen.
  • Extra named schedules and lists, such as “Kids” or “Work”, placed wherever you want in the layout.

Tips

  • Use a shared Google calendar for family events and let it show as the “Everyone” column.
  • New events appear within a few minutes, or tap the heading on the screen to check at once.
  • Skreen can read calendars but can’t change them, so nothing on the wall can be deleted by accident.

Questions

Can Skreen show more than one Google Calendar?

Yes. Free Skreen merges the calendars you tick into one schedule. Skreen+ can also show a separate column for each person.

Can I use more than one Google account?

Yes, with Skreen+. Several Google accounts can feed one screen.

Can Skreen edit my calendar?

No. It can read your calendars, and add or tick off to-dos in Google Tasks, but it can’t create, change or delete events.
