Guide

What’s for dinner? Put the answer on the wall.

A meal plan only helps if everyone can see it without asking you. Skreen+ shows the week’s meals on the kitchen wall tablet, and keeps the plan in a Google Tasks list, so anyone in the family can change it from a phone.

How it works

  1. Make a Google Tasks list called Dinners in the Google Tasks app (or the Tasks panel beside Gmail). The plan stays in your own Google account.
  2. Choose it in Skreen under Settings → Meals & shopping, then tick Dinners under Settings → Layout.
  3. Plan from the wall: tap a day, type or say the meal, and save. Or plan from your phone: add a task to the Dinners list with a date. The task name is the meal and the date is the day it’s for.
  4. Want breakfast and lunch too? Set Meals to plan under Settings → Meals & shopping, and the widget shows every meal for each day.

Pair it with the shopping list

Skreen+ also includes a shopping list on the same screen, synced with Google Tasks. Check off items in the store from your phone and the wall updates within a few minutes.

Good to know

  • Nothing new to sign up for. The plan is stored in your Google Tasks, not on a Skreen server.
  • Meals are part of Skreen+, the one-time $14.99 upgrade. The main display is free.
  • Big Skreen Mode can show the meal plan on a large slide, readable from across the kitchen.

Questions

Where is the meal plan stored?

In a Google Tasks list in your own Google account, so you can edit it from your phone. Skreen doesn’t keep a copy on a server.

Can it show breakfast and lunch as well as dinner?

Yes. Dinner is the default, and breakfast and lunch can be switched on in Settings.

Is the meal planner free?

It is part of Skreen+, a one-time $14.99 purchase (launch price). There is no subscription.
