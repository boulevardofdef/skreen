Guide

A chore chart your kids can tick off on the wall.

Paper charts get lost and magnets fall off. A chore chart on the kitchen wall tablet is always in sight, resets itself each day, and lets kids earn stars toward a reward you choose.

How it works

  1. Add each person and their chores in Settings → Chores.
  2. Say how often each chore repeats: every day, certain days of the week, or every few days.
  3. Switch on the Chores widget in the layout. Each person gets their own block, in their own color.
  4. Kids tap a chore to tick it off. It resets on schedule, so there is nothing to wipe clean.

Stars and rewards (optional)

Turn on stars and give each chore a value. Each person fills a bar toward a reward you set, like movie night or an extra half hour of screen time. At the goal the widget shows Reward ready; tap it to claim. If you don’t like reward systems, leave stars off and use plain check-offs.

Good to know

  • Chores and stars live on the tablet only. They aren’t sent to Google or to Skreen, and there are no accounts. That also means two tablets don’t share a chart.
  • It’s part of Skreen+, the one-time $14.99 upgrade. The main display, with its clock, schedule, weather and to-do list, is free.
  • Big Skreen Mode can rotate chores onto a large slide that is easy to read from across the room.

Questions

Is the chore chart free?

Chores with stars and rewards are part of Skreen+, a one-time $14.99 purchase (launch price) with no subscription. The main Skreen display is free.

Where is chore data stored?

On the tablet only. Skreen doesn’t have accounts or servers for it.

Do I have to use stars?

No. Stars and rewards are optional. You can use plain tick-off chores without them.
