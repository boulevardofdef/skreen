Skreen privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Skreen is a wall display that shows your calendar, a to-do list, the weather and the time. This policy explains what it does with your information. The short version: your Google data goes from Google to your device and nowhere else.

What Skreen accesses

If you sign in with Google, Skreen asks for permission to:

  • Read your Google Calendar (calendar names, colours and events), to show your schedule.
  • Manage your Google Tasks (read, add and complete items), to show and update the to-do list you choose.

Skreen also uses your approximate location, only if you allow it, to show local weather. You can instead type a city.

How it's used and stored

  • Your calendar and task data is fetched directly from Google to your device and is shown on the screen. It is not sent to, or stored on, any server operated by Skreen.
  • Settings (your chosen accounts, calendars, lists, city, calendar links and display preferences) are stored on your device only.
  • If you add a calendar link (for example from Outlook or Apple), Skreen's relay on Cloudflare fetches that link for your device and keeps a copy for about five minutes so repeat requests are quick. The link itself is not stored there.
  • If you use the Skreen+ photo slideshow, your device reads the picture list and pictures of a Google Drive folder you shared with "anyone with the link" directly from Google. Skreen does not see them.
  • If you use the Skreen+ news widget, the relay fetches Wikipedia's "In the news" list and passes it to your device. No information about you is sent.
  • Buying Skreen+ is handled entirely by Google Play. Skreen never sees your payment details.
  • To get the weather, your device sends the coordinates of your chosen location, rounded to about a kilometre, to Skreen's weather relay (hosted on Cloudflare), which passes them to MET Norway, the Norwegian national weather service, and returns the forecast. No account details, and nothing about your calendar or tasks, are sent. Cloudflare may keep standard request logs, such as IP addresses, for a short time.
  • If you search for a city or ZIP code in Settings, the text you type is sent through the same relay to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim search service to find its coordinates.
  • Skreen contains no advertising and no analytics, and does not sell or share your information.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Skreen's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide the features described above, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

Your choices

  • Choose exactly which calendars and which task list Skreen uses in its Settings.
  • Sign out in Skreen's Settings at any time.
  • Revoke access completely in your Google Account under Security → Your connections to third-party apps and services.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device.
