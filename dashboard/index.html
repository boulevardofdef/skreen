        Dinners

        How it works: make a Google Tasks list, such as “Dinners”. Tasks without a date are your meal ideas (add them any time, from your phone too). Tap a day on the Dinners widget and pick an idea, or type a new meal, and Skreen puts a dated copy on that day. You can also add a task to the list with a date, and it shows on that day.
