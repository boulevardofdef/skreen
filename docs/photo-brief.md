# Skreen hero photo: brief

**Where it goes:** the top of skreen.fyi. Save the final image as `site/img/hero-kitchen.jpg` (portrait, 4:5, at least 1600 x 2000 px, under 400 KB if you can). The page picks it up automatically and the illustration underneath disappears. Also useful: a landscape crop (16:9) for social sharing and the Play Store feature graphic.

## The moment
A woman in her thirties, in her own kitchen, making herself lunch. She glances up at the Skreen on the wall, one hand still busy (slicing, stirring, buttering toast). Not posed. Not looking at the camera. A half-second of "right, what's next".

## What we want the viewer to feel
Calm, competent, unhurried. The screen is part of the room, not a gadget on display.

## Composition
- Portrait 4:5. Shot at about shoulder height, slightly off to one side, so we see her profile or three-quarter back, and the tablet on the wall beyond her.
- The tablet is about a hand's width high in the frame but large enough that the big clock and the schedule columns are clearly readable. It is mounted on the wall at eye level or sits on a counter stand, in landscape.
- Natural daylight from a window. Warm, a little golden. Soft shadows.
- Shallow depth of field: she and the screen sharp, the background gently soft.
- Real kitchen details: a cutting board, a bowl of fruit, a mug, a wooden spoon. A little lived-in, not a showroom.

## The screen (important)
Do not photograph a real screen. Reflections and moire ruin it. Composite the real app UI in afterwards:
- Use `site/img/s/main.jpg` (or a freshly captured screenshot at 1920 x 1200, 16:10).
- Show it at the lunchtime hour (about 12:12 PM) so it matches the story.
- Add a faint glow and a very subtle reflection so it feels lit, not pasted on.

## Her
- Early-to-mid thirties. Casual, comfortable clothes (an oatmeal knit, a linen shirt, rolled sleeves). Hair tied up or loose, whatever looks natural.
- Please cast and style with care, and use a real model release.

## Do not
- No smart speaker or other brand's device in frame. No visible logos of other products.
- No stock-photo smiling at the camera. No gadget-reveal energy.
- No second person, no children (keeps the story simple).

## Palette
Warm neutrals (oatmeal, wood, cream). The orange of the Skreen UI (#ff5b2e) is the only strong colour, so a small orange accent in the room (a mug, a dish towel) would tie it together.

## How to get it
1. **Photographer / stock:** hand this brief to a lifestyle photographer, or search licensed stock for "woman kitchen glancing wall screen" and composite our UI onto a tablet-sized blank screen. Check the licence allows commercial use and an app-marketing context.
2. **AI image tool:** if you use one, check its terms for commercial use, and be careful to get natural hands and a believable kitchen. Composite the real UI afterward.
