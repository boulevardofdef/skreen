## Description (2270/4000)
Skreen turns your iPad into an always-on wall display for your day: the time, the weather, what's on the calendar and what's on the list, readable from across the room.

No hardware to buy. Leave an iPad plugged in on the kitchen wall, a stand or a desk, open Skreen, and it's your family's screen.

FREE
- A big clock, weather with an hourly and five-day forecast, and rain warnings
- Your schedule: Google Calendar, plus Outlook, Apple, school and team calendars with a calendar link
- A to-do list that syncs with Google Tasks, so anyone can add or tick things off from a phone
- Timers that keep running, and live internet radio
- A quiet night mode and a layout that shifts slightly through the day to protect the screen
- No ads, no tracking, no Skreen account

SKREEN+ (one-time purchase, no subscription)
- A column for every family member: their own calendars and to-dos, side by side, each in their own color
- Chores that repeat on the days you choose, with optional stars toward a reward
- A sticky-note whiteboard for the household
- A meal planner for dinner (and breakfast and lunch if you like), and a shopping list, both kept in your own Google Tasks
- Several Google accounts, extra named schedules and lists, and your own layout
- Big Skreen Mode: large rotating slides you can read from across the room
- A photo slideshow from your own pictures, or an art gallery

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your calendar and tasks go straight from Google to your iPad. Skreen has no servers that hold them, and no accounts. Chores, notes and photos stay on the iPad. Skreen never listens in the background: the microphone is used only when you tap the microphone button to dictate.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Sign in with Google to see your Google Calendar and Tasks. Without signing in, you can still use calendar links, weather, timers and radio.
- Skreen+ bought on another device or store doesn't carry over; it's tied to your Apple ID. Use Restore Purchase if you've bought it on this Apple ID before.
- Skreen isn't a voice assistant and doesn't control smart-home devices or make calls.
- Music controls for other apps aren't available on iPad. Skreen's own internet radio is.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label") draft: you attest, so check each line
My reading: Skreen's own servers and the app collect no data tied to the user, so the answer would be **Data Not Collected**. Reasons, and what to double-check:
- Google Calendar and Tasks data go from Google to the iPad and are not sent to Skreen.
- Weather: approximate coordinates (about 1 km) pass through a relay to the weather service in real time. Apple counts data as "collected" when it is kept beyond serving the request, and the relay keeps none. Cloudflare may keep short-lived standard request logs (the privacy policy says so). If you'd rather be conservative, declare **Coarse Location**, not linked to the user, not used for tracking, purpose App Functionality.
- Purchases: handled by Apple. Nothing to declare for that.
- Contact info, identifiers, usage data, diagnostics: none collected.
- Tracking: none.
