Skreen puts everything you check ten times a day on one always-on screen: your calendar, to-do list, weather, timers and music. Hang an Android tablet on the kitchen wall (a cheap new one, or the one in your drawer) and retire the whiteboard, the sticky notes and the three different lists.

It's editable from anywhere. Add an event or a task from your phone, laptop or the tablet itself, and it shows up on the wall. Skreen connects to the Google account you already use.

FREE, FOREVER
• Always-on clock and weather you can read from across the room
• Your Google Calendar (plus Outlook, Apple or any calendar link), with what's on right now lit up
• Your Google Tasks to-do list: tap to check things off, add by typing or talking, tag who it's for
• Timers: up to four named timers, set with a keypad or your voice, with a full-screen alarm when they finish
• Music: see what's playing in your music app (such as Spotify or YouTube Music) and pause, play or skip from the wall

Skreen has no accounts of its own. It syncs directly with the Google account you're already using, and your calendar and lists aren't stored on any server of ours. Only your tablet holds them.

BUILT TO BE LEFT ON
Skreen helps protect your screen with a rotating slideshow at an interval you control, a layout that shifts slightly through the day, brightness controls and a quiet night mode.
