## App content

App access: "All or some functionality is restricted". Instructions:
  Skreen shows the user's own Google Calendar and Google Tasks. To review it, sign in with this Google account:
  Email: [TEST ACCOUNT EMAIL - the user fills this in]
  Password: [TEST ACCOUNT PASSWORD - the user fills this in]
  Tap "Sign in with Google" on the first screen. Without signing in, the app shows empty placeholder panels.
  The Music panel needs Android's Notification access; it is optional.

Data safety (use Google's own definitions; if a question doesn't fit, stop and ask the user):
  - Calendar, tasks and settings stay on the device and are not sent to us.
  - Sent to our relay and passed on without being stored: approximate location (rounded to ~1 km) for the weather,
    and the words typed into city search and radio search. If the form counts that as collected, declare
    Approximate location, purpose App functionality, processed ephemerally, not shared for advertising.
  - Approximate location: collection is OPTIONAL (users can choose). Device location is only used if the user taps
    "Use my location" in Settings; otherwise they type a city or ZIP, and only that place's coordinates (rounded to ~1 km)
    are sent. Purpose: App functionality only. Passed through our relay to MET Norway (a service provider for the weather);
    not stored, not used for ads, analytics or personalization. If unsure about "shared", declare it shared with a service provider.
  - Data encrypted in transit: Yes. Users can request deletion: everything is on the device; uninstalling deletes it.
  - No ads, no analytics, no advertising ID.

Notification-access (sensitive permission) reason, if asked:
  Skreen is a wall display. The Music panel shows what the user's music app (Spotify, YouTube Music, etc.) is currently
  playing, with play/pause/skip buttons. It uses the notification-listener permission only to obtain the active media
  session (title, artist, cover art, transport controls). It does not read, store, or transmit notification content, and
  nothing leaves the device. The permission is optional: the user grants it in Android settings and the rest of the app works without it.
